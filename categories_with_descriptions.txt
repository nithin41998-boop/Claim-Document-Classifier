Policy schedule	Document summarising the insured's policy details, including cover, limits, premiums and effective dates.
Claims history	Record of the insured's previous claims history.
Online Vault	Digital storage system where claim-related documents are securely uploaded and kept.
Survey reports	Report from a physical survey or inspection of the insured item, vehicle or property.
Proof of forcible entry	Evidence (photos, police report, or similar) showing that a break-in occurred by force, required to support burglary/theft claims. This includes photos showing visible forced-entry damage such as a splintered or cracked door frame, a broken or dislodged deadbolt/lock strike plate, a pried-open window frame or sash, or a forced/kicked-in door or window. If a photo clearly shows this kind of damage, use this category rather than the generic Photos from clients category, even though the client is the one who submitted it.
Ownership & valuation proof	Documents proving both ownership of the claimed item and its value.
Itemised invoice	An invoice that breaks its charges into specific, individually named line items -- such as particular parts or components (e.g. "Front bumper assembly", "Headlamp assembly"), each with its own description, quantity, and cost -- relating to the claim, regardless of which vendor or repairer issued it. If an invoice instead only groups costs into broad categories like Parts, Labour, and Paint & materials, use Repairer's invoice instead.
Photos from clients	Photos of the damage or loss submitted directly by the client. Use this general category only when no more specific photo category clearly applies -- such as a particular scratch/dent facing direction, the complete damaged panel, proof of forcible entry, or damage submitted for digital assessment. If the photo clearly matches one of those more specific categories, use that instead, even though the client is the one who submitted it.
Supporting claim docs	General supporting documents submitted along with the claim.
Repairer quote	Quotation from a repairer for the cost of fixing the damaged item.
Replacement quote	Quotation for the cost of replacing the damaged or lost item.
Assessed quote	A repair/replacement quote that has been reviewed and approved by a claims assessor.
Authorisation letter	A letter, form, or notice authorising repairs, payment, or another action to proceed on the claim -- including one addressed to a specific named party such as a service provider, as long as it originates or announces the authorisation rather than replying within an ongoing exchange. If the letter explicitly responds to or references a prior communication from that party (e.g. thanking them for their email or query, or replying RE: their message), classify it under that party correspondence category instead, since it is then part of a back-and-forth exchange rather than a standalone authorisation notice.
Authorised AOL	Approved confirmation of the Any One Loss (AOL) amount/excess applicable to the claim.
Signed AOL	Signed acknowledgement by the insured of the Any One Loss (AOL) amount/excess. This includes a signed settlement acceptance (sometimes titled Agreement of Loss) where the insured explicitly ties their acceptance to a stated excess/AOL amount being deducted from the settlement (e.g., wording like less the applicable excess of R X). If the document explicitly states an excess deduction like this, use Signed AOL rather than Signed Release.
Asessement photos	Photos taken during a formal physical assessment visit by an assessor attending in person, showing general assessment context rather than a specific damage angle. If the photo instead clearly shows a scratch or dent from one of the specific facing-direction categories (right, left, upwards, downwards) or the complete damaged panel, use that specific category instead, even if the photo looks professional or workshop-style.
Assessment report	Report prepared by the assessor detailing the damage found and their recommendations.
Investigator's report	Report prepared by a claims investigator on the circumstances of the claim.
Moderator's report	Report from a claims moderator who reviews the assessment/investigation findings.
Incident description / Accident Sketch	Written description and/or diagram of how the incident or accident occurred.
Motor vehicle licence disc	Copy of the vehicle's licence disc (annual registration/roadworthy renewal document).
Transunion retail value	Vehicle valuation (retail value) sourced from TransUnion's Auto Dealers' Guide.
Copy Vehicle Registration Certificate	Copy of the vehicle's registration certificate (NATIS document).
Delivery note for Original Vehicle Registration Certificate	Proof of delivery/receipt of the original vehicle registration certificate.
Signed NCO	Signed Notice of Change of Ownership document for the vehicle.
Finance House Settlement letter	Letter from the financing bank/institution confirming the outstanding settlement balance on the vehicle.
Copy Vehicle De-Registration	Copy of the document confirming the vehicle has been de-registered (e.g. after a total loss).
Unicode	
Tracking Report	Report generated by the vehicle's tracking device provider (e.g. location/movement history).
Credit agreement & statements	Copy of the vehicle finance/credit agreement and related account statements.
Scratch and Dent pre-inspection	Pre-inspection report carried out before a Scratch and Dent (SD) policy/cover starts.
Photo of the scratch or dent facing the right	Photo of a scratch or dent on the vehicle right-hand side. To determine this: find the headlights (front, usually white/clear lenses) and taillights (rear, usually red lenses) in the photo. If the headlights are on the right side of the frame (and taillights on the left, or not visible), the side of the vehicle shown is its right side.
Photo of the scratch or dent facing the left	Photo of a scratch or dent on the vehicle left-hand side. To determine this: find the headlights (front, usually white/clear lenses) and taillights (rear, usually red lenses) in the photo. If the headlights are on the left side of the frame (and taillights on the right, or not visible), the side of the vehicle shown is its left side.
Photo of the scratch or dent facing upwards	Photo of a scratch or dent on an upward-facing surface of the vehicle, such as the roof, bonnet/hood, or boot/trunk lid, typically photographed from above looking down onto the surface.
Photo of the scratch or dent facing downwards	Photo of a scratch or dent on a downward-facing surface or the underside of the vehicle, such as the underbody, sill, or an undercarriage component, typically photographed from below or at a low angle looking up.
Photo of the complete panel that was scratched or dented	Photo showing a single damaged vehicle panel (e.g. one door, one bumper, or one quarter panel) filling most of the frame, giving full context of that one damage location and extent. However, if headlights or taillights are visible in the photo allowing the vehicle side (left/right) or orientation (upward/downward-facing surface) to be determined, use the matching facing-direction category instead of this one, even if a single panel fills the frame.
Photo of the vehicles license disc - Scratch and Dent	Photo of the vehicle's licence disc, submitted specifically for a Scratch and Dent claim.
Service history	Record of the vehicle's past maintenance and servicing.
Photo of vehicle damages for possible digital assessment	A wider photo showing the vehicle overall with damage across multiple panels or areas, or general collision-level damage, rather than a single panel filling the frame. If the photo instead shows just one specific damaged panel, use Photo of the complete panel that was scratched or dented instead.
Photo of the front of the vehicle	Photo where the FRONT end of the vehicle is the main subject: front bumper, bonnet/hood, grille, headlights, front number plate, including front-corner and angled close-ups of the bumper and headlight area, whether damaged or intact. Use this when the picture is dominated by the front end, even if damage is visible there. Only prefer a side scratch/dent category when the side of the vehicle (doors, side panels, wheels) is the main subject.
Photo of the rear of the vehicle	Photo where the REAR end of the vehicle is the main subject: rear bumper, boot/trunk, tailgate, taillights, rear reflectors, rear number plate, including rear-corner and angled close-ups of the bumper and taillight area, whether damaged or intact. Use this when the picture is dominated by the rear end, even if damage is visible there. Only prefer a side scratch/dent category when the side of the vehicle (doors, side panels, wheels) is the main subject.
Itemised list of items claiming for	A detailed list itemising each item being claimed for.
A 1 report	An A1 report - specifically the numbered A1 form, also known as the First Information Report, generated by police immediately after a motor vehicle incident. Only use this category if the document explicitly identifies itself as an A1 form or First Information Report by name or number; otherwise use Saps report.
Alarm Activation Report	Report confirming whether/how an alarm system was activated at the time of the incident.
Confirmation of surge protection	Proof that a surge protection device was installed, relevant to electrical/power-surge related claims.
Club confirmation	Confirmation of the claimant/insured membership with a motoring or roadside-assistance club (e.g. the AA), used to verify eligibility for club-related benefits such as towing or roadside assistance on the claim.
Rental invoice	Invoice for a rental/replacement vehicle used while the insured vehicle is being repaired or replaced.
Water readings & repair evidence	Water meter readings and evidence of repair work, used to support water damage/leak claims.
Proof of rental income loss	Evidence of rental income lost as a result of the insured event.
Repair and leak trace evidence	Evidence of leak detection (trace) work and the subsequent repair carried out.
Catalogue/pricelist value	Value of the claimed item sourced from a catalogue or supplier price list.
Proof of smoke detector	Evidence confirming that a smoke detector was installed at the insured property.
Doctor certifcates	Medical certificate(s) issued by a doctor in support of the claim.
Proof of insured's banking details	Confirmation of the insured's bank account details, used for processing claim payment.
Proof of payment for claim	Evidence that payment for the claim has been made.
No Claim letter	Letter confirming that no claim was made, often used to support a no-claim bonus/discount.
NTU letter	Not Taken Up (NTU) letter, confirming that a policy or offer was not taken up by the insured.
Insured placed on terms letter	Letter notifying the insured that special terms or restrictions have been applied to their policy.
Rejection letter	Letter formally declining/rejecting the claim.
Assessment fees	Fees charged for carrying out the claim assessment.
Expert fees	Fees charged by an expert engaged on the claim.
Expert report	Report prepared by an independent expert or specialist relevant to the claim.
Release invoice	A release invoice (or release receipt) is an official financial document proving that all outstanding fees have been settled, allowing locked property or a pending insurance payment to be released.
Repairer's invoice	Final invoice from the repairer for completed repair work, with costs shown as a single overall charge or grouped only into broad cost categories (such as Parts, Labour, Paint & materials) rather than itemised by specific named part or component. If the repairer's invoice instead breaks costs down into specific individually named line items (e.g. particular parts or components), use Itemised invoice instead.
SP repair/replacement  invoices	Invoices from the service provider (SP) for repair or replacement work carried out.
Signed clearance	Signed clearance/discharge document confirming the claim or matter is settled.
Certificate - Roadworthy	Roadworthy certificate confirming the vehicle is fit and safe for road use.
Certificate - Vehicle Inspection	Certificate issued following a formal vehicle inspection.
Saps report	A general case, accident, or statement report or extract obtained from the South African Police Service (SAPS) - including case docket extracts and statement summaries. This is the default SAPS-document category; use it unless the document explicitly identifies itself as the specific numbered A1 / First Information Report form.
Call recordings	Recorded telephone calls relevant to the claim.
Broker correpondence	Correspondence, emails, messages actually addressed to or sent from the insurance broker (based on sender/recipient, not names merely mentioned in the body).
Client correpondence	Correspondence, emails, messages actually addressed to or sent from the client/insured (based on sender/recipient, not names merely mentioned in the body).
Service provider correpondence	Correspondence, emails, or messages exchanged with a service provider, insurer, or related party -- including repair/vendor communication on a claim, as well as general policy, payment, or renewal correspondence with an insurance company.
Assessor correpondence	Correspondence, emails, messages actually addressed to or sent from the claims assessor (based on sender/recipient, not names merely mentioned in the body).
Investigator correpondence	Correspondence, emails, messages actually addressed to or sent from the claims investigator (based on sender/recipient, not names merely mentioned in the body).
Moderator correpondence	Correspondence, emails, messages exchanged with the claims moderator.
Salvage correpondence	Correspondence, emails, messages relating to the salvage of the damaged item/vehicle.
Sasria correpondence	Correspondence, emails, messages actually addressed to or sent from SASRIA, the South African insurer covering riot, strike and public disorder damage (based on sender/recipient, not names merely mentioned in the body).
Ombudsman correpondence	Correspondence, emails, messages with the Insurance Ombudsman regarding a claim dispute.
TP docs	General documents relating to the third party (TP) involved in the claim.
TP summons	Legal summons issued to or by the third party (TP).
TP release	Documentation relating to the third party (TP) release/discharge of the claim -- including both the signed release/discharge document itself, and any covering letter or communication to the third party requesting them to sign and return that release.
TP assessment quote	Repair/replacement quote obtained for the third party's (TP) vehicle or property.
TP assessment report	Assessment report covering the third party's (TP) vehicle or property.
Acknowledgement of debt	Signed document in which a party formally acknowledges they owe a debt.
Trace Report	If a vehicle has been stolen, the insurer will not process a total-loss payout immediately. They require a formal No Trace Report (or Non-Traceable Certificate) from the police confirming the vehicle could not be located.
Attorney Instruction	Formal letter instructing an attorney to act on the claim/matter.
Release Offer	Formal offer made to settle and release a claim.
Signed Release	Signed release/settlement agreement finalising the claim, where the insured or third party accepts a final settlement without tying that acceptance to a stated Any One Loss (AOL) excess deduction. If the acceptance explicitly states an excess/AOL amount being deducted from the settlement (e.g., less the applicable excess of R X), use Signed AOL instead.
Trace fees	Fees charged for the vehicle tracing/investigation service.
Attorneys fees	Legal fees charged by an attorney.
Witness Request	Formal request for a witness statement to be provided.
Witness Statement	Written statement provided by a witness to the incident.
Summons	Legal summons document relating to the claim.
Salvage invoice	Invoice for the sale of salvaged (written-off) property.
Passport	Copy of the insured or claimant's passport, used as proof of identity.
Drivers License	Copy of the insured or claimant's driver's licence.
Photo ID	Copy of a photo identification document.
Insurance Claim	A general insurance claim document or communication that does not fit a more specific category above.
Lottery Win	A message or notice claiming the recipient has won a lottery, sweepstake or prize, often requesting personal or payment details -- a common scam pattern.
Tax Notice	A notice or letter presenting itself as being from a tax authority (e.g. IRS or equivalent), regarding an assessment, refund or payment.
Package Delivery Notice	A notice presenting itself as being about a package or parcel delivery, often requesting a fee or personal details to release the item.
Visa Approval	A letter or notice presenting itself as approving a visa or travel authorisation application.
System Security Alert	A notice presenting itself as a security, virus or antivirus alert, often prompting immediate action, payment or remote access.
Job Offer Letter	A letter or message presenting itself as a job offer, often requesting personal, banking or identity details as part of an onboarding process.
Charitable Donation Request	A message requesting a charitable donation, often relating to a disaster, emergency or cause.
Claim approval	Letter or notice confirming that the claim has been approved for payment or settlement.
Claim Form/application	The initial form or application submitted by the insured to formally lodge/register the claim.
